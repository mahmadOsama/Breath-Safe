Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last Updated: January 2025 5 min read

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Details Username, email, phone, password
  • Profile Info Name, preferences, settings
  • API Tokens Authentication credentials

Device & Technical Data

  • Device Fingerprint Browser, resolution, hardware
  • IP Address Security monitoring only
  • Session Data Login times, activity logs

Sensor & Safety Data

  • Gas Readings MQ6, MQ7 sensor data
  • Environmental Temperature, humidity levels
  • Alert History Warning and alert records

How We Use Your Data

Safety Monitoring

Real-time gas leak detection and environmental monitoring to keep you safe

Account Security

Preventing unauthorized access through device fingerprinting and verification

Alert Notifications

Sending critical safety alerts when gas levels exceed safe thresholds

Service Improvement

Analyzing usage patterns to enhance system reliability and accuracy

Customer Support

Providing technical assistance and responding to your inquiries

Legal Compliance

Meeting regulatory requirements and responding to lawful requests

Data Security Measures

End-to-End Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 protocol.

Device Authentication

Advanced device fingerprinting ensures only authorized devices can access your account.

Brute Force Protection

Automatic account lockout after multiple failed login attempts prevents unauthorized access.

Secure Infrastructure

Data stored on Google Firebase with enterprise-grade security and automatic backups.

Your Rights

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you within 30 days

Right to Rectification

Update or correct any inaccurate information through your account settings

Right to Erasure

Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data

Right to Portability

Export your data in a machine-readable format for personal use

Right to Object

Opt out of certain data processing activities and marketing communications
