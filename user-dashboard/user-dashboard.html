Emergency Guidelines

Gas Leak Emergency Critical safety steps
  • Ventilate Immediately Open all windows and doors
  • Avoid Sparks DO NOT use electrical switches
  • No Phones Inside Call from outside only
  • Shut Off Gas Turn off main valve if safe
  • Evacuate Now Leave and call 911 outside
High Temperature Alert Heat emergency response
  • Increase Airflow Turn on fans and ventilation
  • Check Appliances Disconnect overheating devices
  • Monitor Readings Watch temperature continuously
  • Prepare Safety Gear Keep extinguisher accessible
  • Alert Others Inform all occupants
Fire Emergency Fire safety protocol
  • Sound the Alarm Activate fire alarm immediately
  • Find Exit Route Use nearest safe exit
  • Stay Low Avoid smoke inhalation
  • Check Doors Feel with back of hand first
  • Call 911 Once safely outside

Daily Safety Tips

Ventilate your space daily by opening windows for at least 15 minutes.

Check gas hoses and connections regularly for cracks or damage.

Keep humidity below 60% to prevent mold and maintain air quality.

Clean heating filters monthly during heavy use periods.

Test detectors monthly and replace batteries every 6 months.
